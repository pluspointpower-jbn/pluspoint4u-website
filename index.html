Message from the CMD

At Plus Point Group of Education, we believe that education is not only about academic achievement; it is also about building character, confidence, discipline and a sense of responsibility. Our aim is to provide every learner with an environment in which they can discover their potential, develop strong values and prepare for a brighter future.

I encourage all students to work hard, stay disciplined, remain curious and pursue excellence with honesty and determination. With the support of our dedicated teachers and the trust of parents, we will continue striving to help every child learn, grow and succeed.

R. M. SangwanCMD, Plus Point Group of Education
